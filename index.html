Discover the Borden family story

The Battle of Prairie Grove was fought on December 7, 1862. For families living in the area, the battle was not simply a military event. It unfolded around their homes, farms, and communities.

Archibald Borden and his family were among the civilians whose lives were affected by the conflict. Historical records, family recollections, and local accounts offer pieces of their story. Your task is to examine the evidence and identify the documented family connections.

How to investigate

Begin by examining the historical evidence. Open each record, study the document image, and look for names and relationships. Then use the family cards to build the family group. A shared surname does not necessarily mean two people are related.
